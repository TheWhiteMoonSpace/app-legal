Privacy Policy

App: cyber-shorts-uploader  •  Effective date: 4 September 2026

cyber-shorts-uploader ("the app") is a personal command-line tool used by its developer to publish their own short-form videos to their own social media accounts through official platform APIs (the YouTube Data API and the TikTok Content Posting API). It is not a consumer product and is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

  • OAuth tokens for the developer's own YouTube and TikTok accounts, obtained through each platform's standard consent flow.
  • Video files and their metadata (title, description, tags, caption file, thumbnail) that the developer explicitly chooses to upload.
  • Basic account information returned by the APIs (for example the channel or creator username, and the list of allowed privacy settings) used only to complete an upload.

How data is stored and used

  • Tokens are stored locally in a .env file on the developer's own computer. The app has no server, no database, and no hosted component.
  • Data is used solely to authenticate to the platform APIs and to upload the developer's own content.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used for analytics or profiling.
  • The app does not collect data from any other users. It has no other users.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored tokens persist until the developer deletes the local files or revokes the app's access. You can revoke access at any time:

  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions

Data security

Because the app runs only on the developer's machine and transmits data only to Google and TikTok over HTTPS, exposure is limited to that single device. Credentials are kept out of version control.

Children

The app is not directed to children and processes no data about children.
